# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience,
nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and scientific traditions
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Properly citing astronomical sources and prior work
- Acknowledging indigenous knowledge systems with respect

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Misrepresenting or appropriating indigenous astronomical knowledge

## Academic Integrity

- Cite all data sources (ephemeris, archaeological records, peer-reviewed papers)
- Distinguish between scientific calculation and symbolic interpretation
- Acknowledge indigenous communities whose knowledge is referenced

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to:
`rafaelmeloreisnovo@gmail.com`

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
